# Phase 0 — Red-Flag List v1 (draft, REQUIRES clinician review before build)

Educational triage bands for `Is This Normal?` (rules engine input). These are patterns that warrant talking to a trusted adult / clinic — NEVER a diagnosis.

| # | Pattern (structured inputs) | Outcome band | Message direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Soaking pad/cloth in under 2 hours, or bleeding over 7 days | See professional soon | Heavy bleeding needs a check; prepare 3 dates + flow notes |
| R2 | Severe pain (4–5/5) + missed school / vomiting / fainting | See professional soon | Pain that stops life needs a check |
| R3 | Cycles under 21 days or over 45 days, repeating 3+ times (age-adjusted; first 1–2 years can be irregular) | Talk to trusted adult | Pattern worth discussing, bring logs |
| R4 | Discharge with bad smell + itching/burning/fever, or green/yellow | See professional soon | Possible infection signs need a check |
| R5 | No period by 16, or period stops 3+ months (not pregnancy-related context for this age group — handle sensitively) | Talk to trusted adult | Worth a clinic conversation |
| R6 | Sudden severe one-sided pain, dizziness/fainting, very heavy sudden bleed | Urgent — clinic/emergency now | Emergency wording + tell adult immediately |

Default (no flags): monitor at home + self-care + what to watch for + when to return.
