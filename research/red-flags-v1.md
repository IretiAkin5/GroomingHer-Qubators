# Support content review — replacing the former triage rules

Automated symptom triage, disease-risk scores, cycle pattern alerts and diary-powered AI are excluded from the first build. The former red-flag rule table is superseded, not approved clinical guidance.

A healthcare reviewer and child-protection adviser must approve lesson content, general care guidance, urgent/unsafe-situation responses and safe human referral routes. Tests must include personal diagnosis requests, medicines, intimate images, severe pain and abuse-related prompts. No urgent response may ask someone to wait for an app reply. Do not invent thresholds or publish unreviewed clinical rules.

The demonstration contains labelled sample learning and generic immediate-human-help routes. AI remains unavailable. No personal health information is collected. See [PRD sections 9–10](../GroomingHer_PRD.md).
