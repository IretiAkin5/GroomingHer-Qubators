# Phase 0 — Teen Interview Guide (GroomingHer)

Goal: validate vocabulary, phone/data reality, and top "is this normal?" questions. 5–8 interviews, ages 12–19, Nigeria. 20–30 min. Guardian consent required for under-18.

## Setup
- Explain: learning project, no medical advice, can skip any question, can stop anytime.
- No names in notes — use codes (T01, T02…). Store notes in `/research/notes/`.

## Questions
1. Phone: Whose phone do you use? Shared with who? Data — daily limit? What apps work offline?
2. Period: How do you track your period today (memory, paper, app)? What is hardest about knowing when it comes?
3. Symptoms: What body changes worry you most? (prompt only if needed: pain, flow, discharge, acne, missed school)
4. Help-seeking: Last time you worried — who did you ask first and why? What stopped you asking a parent/teacher/nurse?
5. Words: What words do you use for period, discharge, cramps? What words feel embarrassing?
6. Trust: What would make you trust an app with these questions? What would make you delete it immediately?
7. Parent: If the app could help you tell a parent, what would that help look like (words to say, a note to show)?

## Red lines
- Do not ask for photos, do not request explicit detail, do not give medical opinions.
- If she reports severe symptoms (fainting, very heavy bleeding, severe pain, fever), encourage telling a trusted adult/clinic promptly and note it (no diagnosis).
