# Voluntary feedback guide — girls aged 13–15

Use the approved [PRD](../GroomingHer_PRD.md) and appropriate guardian permission/girl agreement before involving children. No research session is authorised by this document. Health and child-protection guidance must be reviewed first.

Explore fictional tasks: find a lesson; explain the Christian reflection versus health explanation; skip the diary; edit a fictional entry; cancel a message; find a safe adult and backup. Ask what is clear/confusing and whether the language feels respectful. Ask about broad device/print access preferences, not personal medical history.

Do not request real diaries, intimate details, symptom disclosures, names of other children or images. Participation and activities are voluntary. Discuss confidential handling and a reviewed protection procedure before sessions. A serious disclosure requires the agreed human process, not a dashboard record.
