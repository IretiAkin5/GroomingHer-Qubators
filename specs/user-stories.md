# Current user stories and acceptance criteria

[The founder-supplied PRD 3.1](../GroomingHer_PRD.md) is the authoritative inventory and acceptance list. The former triage-based stories are superseded.

## Initial demonstration targets
- LAND-01–08: exact public navigation, three Solutions destinations, complete ordered landing sections, mobile menu and no fabricated service claims.
- PUB05: public sample resources are readable without signup and labelled awaiting professional review. This preview is an explicit sample exception; no sample is claimed published/approved.
- ONB-G/P/S: brief fictional role journeys; optional preferences; no health-history collection; permissions separate and not preselected; school interest remains pending.
- GIR01/02/05: learning-first home, six lessons, distinct faith reflections and fictional learning checks; support independent of diary use.
- GIR06–09: optional fixed fictional diary CRUD; exact recipient/message preview; cancelled sharing sends nothing; no automatic attachments; simulated failures are truthful.
- PAR01–08: practical guides, only girl-confirmed sample messages, programme and support; no diary or private questions.
- SCH01–09: schedules, materials, readiness, fictional participation and attendance; only combined learning feedback with small-group suppression.
- Cross-feature: no personal health input or backend collection; role guard; exit clears temporary demo state; accessible phone and desktop layouts.

Live-account security, professional content approvals, real support availability and pilot readiness are not satisfied by a demonstration. See [implementation stages](../GroomingHer_Implementation_Plan.md) and [build evidence](../docs/BUILD_STAGES.md).
