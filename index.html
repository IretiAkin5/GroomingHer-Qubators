The journey

I have a question → I ask GroomingHer → I understand → I know what to do next → I know who to talk to.

What it does

  • 💬 Private “Ask Her” companion + “Is This Normal?” guidance
  • 👪 Parent Space — consent-only sharing, conversation guides
  • 🔒 PIN lock, discreet mode, offline-friendly
